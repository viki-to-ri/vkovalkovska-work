## Context

rove.me is a travel guide built around timing — helping people figure out the best time to visit a destination based on what they actually want to do there, not just when flights are cheap. It blends editorial content with data on seasonality, weather, and crowd levels.

## Goal

Increase engagement, retention, and bookings by making the site's content and features easier to discover.

## My Role

As the sole product designer, I generated hypotheses for improvement, designed the UX/UI changes from sketch through hi-fi prototype, wrote the UX copy, collaborated with engineering on implementation, and designed and analyzed the A/B tests that validated — or killed — each idea.

## Constraints

The team had limited resources to build and test ideas, so every experiment had to be cheap to build and fast to read.

## Process

I optimized for experimentation and fast learning: reviewing analytics, competitor sites, and recorded user behavior; turning that into hypotheses; redesigning interactive features like a dynamic graph tooltip; and running A/B tests to validate impact before iterating further.

## Results

- Unique visitors up 23%
- Returning visitors up 17%
- Bookings up 14%
- The redesigned dynamic tooltip alone lifted pages per session by 12%
