subline: "0→1 concept · travel discovery via podcasts"
standfirst: >-
  A 0→1 concept and MVP testing whether curated, place-specific podcasts could be
  a real way to start planning a trip. Built with iHeartMedia's research team.
role: "Sole product designer"
when: "Jun – Aug 2021"
tools: "Figma, UserTesting"
ledger:
  - label: "CONTEXT"
    body: >-
      Research showed travellers already turn to podcasts for inspiration, but discovery was
      scattered across podcast apps with no travel-specific way in. Nobody knew whether that gap
      was an opportunity or just a habit that worked fine as it was.
  - label: "GOAL"
    body: >-
      Find out whether location-specific, curated podcasts could be a genuine starting point for
      planning a trip — by shipping an MVP ahead of peak travel season and watching real behaviour,
      rather than researching the question further.
  - label: "WHAT I DID"
    body: >-
      Sole designer on a small cross-functional team, covering research, concept, prototyping,
      testing and interface design. Validated the concept early · iterated fast from low to high
      fidelity · ran frequent co-creation sessions · used usability testing to sharpen or discard
      assumptions. Worked directly with engineering and partnered with iHeartMedia's research team
      throughout.
  - label: "HARD PART"
    body: >-
      The idea only works if the podcasts themselves are worth listening to — which is a curation
      problem, not a design one. Designing around that meant keeping the MVP deliberately thin, so
      what we measured was whether people wanted the thing at all, not whether I had built a
      convincing shell around a thin library.
  - label: "OUTCOME"
    highlight: true
    body: >-
      Shipped a lightweight MVP that put the concept in front of real users before the season
      peaked. I rolled off after launch; the team carried the insights into the next iteration.
