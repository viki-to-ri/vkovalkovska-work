## Context

Research showed travelers already turn to podcasts for inspiration, but there was no good way to discover them by destination — discovery was scattered across podcast apps with no travel-specific entry point. iHeartMedia's research team and our product team wanted to know whether location-specific, curated podcasts could become a genuinely useful starting point for planning a trip.

## Goal

Validate whether podcasts could work as a travel discovery mechanism by shipping a lightweight MVP, testing the core assumptions, and watching how people actually used it — ahead of the busy travel season.

## My Role

I was the sole product designer on a small cross-functional team, covering the full arc from research and concept to prototyping, testing, and interface design, working directly with engineering and partnering closely with iHeartMedia's research division throughout.

## Process

I optimized for speed and learning: validating the concept early, iterating fast from low to high fidelity, running frequent co-creation and feedback sessions, and using usability testing to sharpen or kill assumptions along the way.

## Results

We shipped a lightweight MVP to test the concept with real users. After launch, I handed the project off to the team, who kept gathering insights to shape the next iteration.
