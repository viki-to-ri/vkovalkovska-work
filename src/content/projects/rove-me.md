  - label: "CONTEXT"
    body: >-
      rove.me is a travel guide built around timing — the best moment to visit somewhere based on
      what you actually want to do there, not on when flights are cheapest. Editorial writing sits
      on top of data about seasonality, weather and how crowded a place gets.
  - label: "GOAL"
    body: >-
      Increase engagement, retention and bookings by making the content and features easier
      to discover.
  - label: "WHAT I DID"
    body: >-
      Sole designer in a cross-functional team. Generated the improvement hypotheses · designed
      from sketch through hi-fi prototype · wrote the UX copy · worked with engineering on
      implementation · designed and analysed the A/B tests that validated or killed each idea.
  - label: "HARD PART"
    body: >-
      There was almost no resource to build with, so every idea had to be cheap to ship and fast
      to read. That ruled out anything resembling a redesign and pushed me toward small changes on
      high-traffic surfaces, where even a modest lift is measurable. The dynamic graph tooltip was
      the clearest case: a single interaction, rebuilt, tested on its own.
  - label: "OUTCOME"
    highlight: true
    body: >-
      Unique visitors **↑23%** · returning visitors **↑17%** · bookings **↑14%**. The tooltip test
      on its own lifted pages per session by 12%, and confirmed that discoverability — not the
      content itself — had been the constraint.
